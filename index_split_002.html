Clues

Let us list the clues that we have available. First, some from Oral Tradition:

  • The Major Arcana holds a deep secret.
  • The Tarot is the Devil's picture book, and
  • In order to understand the Tarot, you must first have the key.
  • The Major Arcana is sometimes referred to as the Journey of the Fool, or the Journey of the Soul.

An often-overlooked clue is:

  • The full name of the organisation is The HERMETIC order of the Golden Dawn, so perhaps the answer lies within the hermetic school of thought.

To these we can add some solid historical evidence:

  • The Tarot evolved over time, Starting with Roman mythology cards called Trionfi, starting around 1420 in Italy, and taking advantage of the newly invented Gutenberg printing press.
  • France invaded Italy, and “took” the cards, eventually achieving a measure of standardisation in the Marseilles deck, published 1639. By this time, the essential structure of the deck and cards was already greatly similar to the Waite deck, and so, if a secret was added to them, it must have been done prior to this date. Thus, we have a date range for the encoding of this secret. 1420 to 1639.

  • Does God or Gods exist?
  • Early documents of this nature are known by the name Creation Myths, of which there are many, such as Genesis. A creation myth would surely fit the bill for a secret within the Majors.
