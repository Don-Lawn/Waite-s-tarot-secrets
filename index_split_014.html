The Emperor

Representation

Mars, God of war, as Emperor

Meaning

The violent side of the male, and consorting with the wife of Vulcan

Opposite Card

The Tower, punishment for Hubris.

Function

Identifies Mars

The Emperor is the Governor Mars (aka Ares), the God of War. The ram heads are symbols of the zodiac sign of Aries, symbolised by the Ram and ruled by Mars. The Emperor takes rulership by force.

[P13]       The Demiurge made the seven planetary Governors from the elements of nature. These seven encompass the entire world that is perceived by the senses, and their rule is called Fate.

[…Mars=The Emperor, …]

RWS Imagery

A man holding a staff is seated on a stone throne bearing ram’s heads. He wears a gold crown, a red robe, and boots of armour. The background of the card is red/orange in colour. His right hand carries a gold sceptre of ruler-ship, and his left holds an orb, symbol of world domination, or empire. His throne is adorned with ram’s heads. His boots, and possibly his arms, are from a medieval suit of armour, which is mostly hidden by his red robes. This mirrors the way in which he may claim to rule by divine right, but in truth his only right is the might of arms.
