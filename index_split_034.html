Physical Descriptions

However, these descriptions are clearly rooted in a white European tradition and do not map well to other ethnicities or cultures.

The Pymander approach avoids these limitations by basing its face-card imagery on MBTI types rather than physical traits. The theory is that different MBTI types exhibit characteristic facial expressions or micro-expressive patterns, allowing personality to be inferred from the face rather than from racialised physical features. Each Pymander face card is therefore a digital composite of several famous individuals whose sex and MBTI type match the card in question (based on published assessments by typology “experts,” acknowledging that some inconsistencies exist between sources).
