Kings — NT Types (Fire, Hot and Dry)

Kings are the NT types — intuitive thinkers. They prefer to work in the abstract and focus on systems, strategy, and long-range outcomes rather than emotional considerations. As Fire types, they see the big picture within their suit and set direction at the highest level. Kings think in terms of principles, frameworks, and long-term utility.

Kings work at the abstract, strategic level to move the kingdom forward in a utilitarian sense. They are the captains of industry, the architects of systems, and the ones who define the rules rather than follow them.

Kings correspond to the D type in DISC analysis. They are rare, making up only 12% of the population.

Queens — NF Types (Air, Hot and Wet)

Queens are the NF types — intuitive feelers. They also work in the abstract, but their focus is on people, meaning, values, and social cohesion. As Air types, they see the big picture in terms of relationships, ethics, and collective wellbeing.

Queens work at the abstract, interpersonal level to move the kingdom forward in a collaborative sense. They provide social structure, moral leadership, and the connective tissue that binds communities together.

Queens correspond to the I type in DISC analysis. They are rare, making up only 12% of the population.

Knights — ST Types (Earth, Cold and Dry)

Knights are the ST types — sensory thinkers. They prefer to work in the concrete and focus on results, efficiency, and practical execution. As Earth types, they respond to what their senses tell them and excel at detail, structure, and reliability.

Knights work at the practical, material level to move the kingdom forward in a utilitarian sense. They are the workers of industry, the implementers, the ones who build, maintain, and enforce.

Knights correspond to the C type in DISC analysis. They make up 38% of the population.

It is useful to think of Earth here as the structured nature of crystal, rather than the amorphous nature of soil.

Pages — SF Types (Water, Cold and Wet)

Pages are the SF types — sensory feelers. They work in the concrete but focus on people, emotions, and interpersonal harmony. As Water types, they respond to the emotional tone of their environment and excel in one-to-one interactions.

Pages work at the practical, interpersonal level to improve the general happiness and wellbeing of the kingdom. They are the heart of the community, the helpers, carers, and companions.

Pages correspond to the S type in DISC analysis. They make up 38% of the population.

The MBTI Axes

The MBTI categorises people by assessing their polarity on four axes:

1. Extroversion Axis (E/I)

This axis describes where a person draws their energy from.

  • Extroverts (E) feel energised by social interaction.
  • Introverts (I) find prolonged interaction draining and must withdraw to recharge.

2. Perception Axis (S/N)

This axis describes how people take in information.

  • Sensing (S) types focus on the information provided by their senses — the concrete, material world.
  • iNtuitive (N) types look for patterns, meanings, and abstract relationships.

3. Decision-Making Axis (T/F)

This axis describes how people evaluate information and make choices.

  • Thinking (T) types prioritise logic, structure, and objective criteria.
  • Feeling (F) types prioritise values, harmony, and the emotional impact of decisions.

4. Lifestyle Axis (J/P)

This axis describes how people interact with the outer world.

  • Judging (J) types prefer structure, closure, and predictability.
  • Perceiving (P) types prefer flexibility, openness, and adaptability.

Why the Simple Mapping Fails

The traditional “N = Fire, T = Air, F = Water, S = Earth” mapping treats each MBTI letter as if it corresponds to a single element. But MBTI types are defined by four letters, and the elements are defined by two axes (Hot/Cold and Wet/Dry). A one-letter mapping cannot capture the full structure.

For example:

  • NT types are Hot + Dry → Fire
  • NF types are Hot + Wet → Air
  • SF types are Cold + Wet → Water
  • ST types are Cold + Dry → Earth

This produces the correct elemental quadrants and aligns perfectly with population statistics, DISC, and the behavioural patterns of the court cards.
