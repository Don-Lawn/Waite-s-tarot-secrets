About the Author

Don was born in Rockhampton, Queensland in 1959 and graduated with a degree in Physics in 1980. That same year, while settling into life and work in Canberra, a housemate introduced him—rather cryptically—to the Tarot. Intrigued but denied access to the deck, Don did what any self-respecting physicist would do: he bought his own. And a book. Then another deck. More books. Each answered parts of the puzzle—but none explained why the cards meant what they did. Worse still, each author contradicted the last.

Thus began a lifelong quest—not for the cards themselves, but for the logic behind them. The Ace of Cups became his personal Holy Grail.

After thirty years of wandering through the worlds of occultism, symbology, and esoteric traditions, Don stumbled on a revelation. In 2012, while reading Roman mythology alongside The Divine Pymander of Hermes Trismegistus, he recognized four Major Arcana cards embedded in the Pymander story. That flash of insight sparked a deeper unravelling. Over the next decade, piece by piece, the Tarot revealed itself not as chaos, but as code—a layered symbolic system rooted in historical logic.

  • The Major Arcana are a pictorial representation of the Hermetic creation myth.
  • The Court Cards trace philosophical descent from Aristotle’s elements, through the medieval theory of Humours, mapped via Jungian archetypes to the Myers-Briggs MBTI types.
  • The Minor Arcana reflect decan-based astrological structures.

Together, the cards form a composite of three integrated systems—magickal, philosophical, and psychological. His digital Tarot composition is both artwork and investigative artefact.

Now, in 2025, Don is finalizing this body of knowledge as an e-book for release on Amazon Kindle. His hope: not merely to explain Tarot, but to reveal its structural DNA—what centuries of commentary have obscured, and what myth, astrology, philosophy, and psychology still remember.

He married Daniela in 1997, and credits her unwavering patience as the silent cornerstone of this entire undertaking.
